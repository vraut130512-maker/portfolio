About Me

I'm a final-year Computer Science Engineering student at Nutan College of Engineering and Research, Talegaon, Pune, affiliated with Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University. My interest sits at the intersection of secure systems and practical software — building things that work reliably and protect the data passing through them.

Before this, I completed a Diploma in Information Technology at Government Polytechnic, Amravati. Across both, I've stayed hands-on: writing the encryption logic myself, wiring up the databases myself, and debugging the edge cases myself. I learn fast and I like finishing what I start.

Outside of coursework, I build full-stack side projects end to end — from a lead-management dashboard with role-based auth to a real-time face-recognition attendance system — and I keep my problem-solving sharp on LeetCode.
